# Epistemic Policy

VOXIS research artifacts must distinguish the following classes:

- **Observation** — what an operator directly notices or records.
- **Measurement** — quantified geometry, transform, residual, count, density, or other reproducible output.
- **Inference** — a relationship proposed from observations/measurements.
- **Interpretation** — semantic, symbolic, procedural, or functional reading.
- **Speculation** — exploratory possibility not yet supported enough for inference.

## Non-merging rule

A later class may reference an earlier class but must not replace it. Raw source geometry and operator wording remain preserved.

## Alignment rule

A visually interesting configuration is not automatically an alignment, correspondence, evidence item, or manuscript meaning. Exact transformations and residuals must be retained, and negative/null results are first-class research outputs.
