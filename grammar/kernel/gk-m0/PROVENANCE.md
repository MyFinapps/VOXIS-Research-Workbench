## Blake tri-folio boundary

The current provisional geometric master reports recoverable transforms for 2v and horizontally flipped 3r, while 2r remains unresolved from the documentary photograph. Blake's interpretation of 2v as earth/dirt covering roots is preserved as interpretation, not measurement.

GK-M0 therefore treats any 2r/2v/3r grammar event as provisional until the relevant geometry is independently measured or human-validated.

## Non-negotiable validation rules

- Geometric fit is a research aid, not proof of manuscript correspondence.
- Visual similarity alone is not a positive result.
- Exact transformation parameters must be preserved.
- Every fit must report residuals or another declared error measure.
- Interpretation may reference geometry but may not overwrite it.
- Negative and null results are retained.
- A canonical event may be replayed in VR or IF without changing its epistemic class.
